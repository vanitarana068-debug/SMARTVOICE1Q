# Brief

## Intent

The person supplied a 5-panel marketing image for "Smart Invoice & Follow-Up App" and asked for
"this exactly in something that moves". The video animates those five panels in order, keeping their
copy, numbers, colours and layout, and links them with transitions caused by objects on screen.

Panels, in order (copy verbatim from the image):

1. Smart Invoice & Follow-Up App: laptop dashboard + phone "Create Invoice" form, four feature icons, "Simplify Your Billing" CTA. Navy/blue.
2. Create Invoices in Seconds: checklist, tilted invoice (₹12,500) with SENT stamp and paper plane. Blue.
3. Track Payment Status: status legend + Payment Status table, "No more missed payments". Dark teal-green.
4. Automate Polite Reminders: checklist + phone reminder for #INV-004, "Polite. Automated. Effective." Indigo/purple.
5. Get Paid Faster: checklist + rising green bar chart with ₹ coin, "Grow Your Business". Teal-green.

Then an end card: app icon + name + "Simplify Your Billing" button.

## Customizations

- Destination: website / YouTube, 16:9, 1920×1080 at 30 fps (person's choice).
- Built straight through, no storyboard stop (person's choice).

## Notes

- Sound: the brief is a ~28 s promo, which by default gets music. No music source is signed in on this
  machine (`hyperframes usage` → unsupported_auth), so the film ships silent; see REVIEW.md.
- The UI in the panels is the person's marketing mockup (sample data such as ABC Traders), reproduced
  as given at their request. It is not the live app's UI.
